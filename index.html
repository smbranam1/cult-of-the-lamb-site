Cult of the Lamb

This site is about Cult of the Lamb, a fun game by Massive Monster and Devolver Digital about expanding your flock, defeating heretics, and overthrowing the bishops of the Old Faith.
Here is one cool fact: very real and legal Cult of the Lamb-themed weddings were hosted at PAX in Melbourne, Australia in 2024 by the developers/publisher of the game.
